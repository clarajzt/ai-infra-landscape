About this map

An independent research map of the AI infrastructure stack, from chips and GPU clouds to agent runtimes, evals, identity and payments. It is built to show where capital, revenue and consolidation are moving, segment by segment, with every figure traceable to a public source.

How to use it

  • Market overview shows the stack layer by layer with headline funding, revenue, valuation and M&A, a landscape of leading companies, and a yearly trend. Every number opens the observations behind it.
  • Company map lists every company and product with country, segment, product, and its latest public figures. Click a company for its full history.
  • Evolution and opportunities sets out how each generation of model capability moved the scarce resource, and where the investable seams are.
  • Filter by year, country, layer and segment, or search. The address bar keeps your place, so any view can be bookmarked or shared. Export downloads the current selection as CSV.

How to read the numbers

  • This is a research sample, not a census. A company or deal that is missing has not been recorded; it is not a zero.
  • Every observation carries its source type, from regulatory filings to third-party estimates. Estimates and media reports are included and labelled, so coverage is broad, but check the linked source before relying on a single figure.
  • Funding counts announced equity rounds by announcement date. Total-raised snapshots, debt facilities, IPO proceeds and secondary sales are shown but not counted as new funding.
  • Revenue keeps the original basis. ARR, annualized run-rate and recognized revenue are different measures and are labelled. Revenue totals add up the disclosed sample; they are not segment revenue.
  • Valuations are shown as medians, never summed.
  • M&A counts each deal once, by announcement date. Signing and closing are tracked separately.
  • Currency: amounts are in USD. RMB is converted at a fixed 7 CNY/USD and EUR at 1.17 USD/EUR for analysis; these are not historical exchange rates.
  • Shares and growth describe the recorded sample. They are not market share, and new disclosures entering the sample are not treated as industry growth.

Coverage and limits

  • 175 entries are discovery records that are not yet classified. They stay in the company map but out of the landscape totals.
  • Directory "total raised" figures are third-party and unverified, and can be badly wrong. They are shown on company profiles but never counted as funding.
  • Coverage is strongest for agent and developer infrastructure. Chips, power and data centres are covered only where they bear on that stack.
  • Each company sits in one segment, so multi-product companies are filed under their main business.
